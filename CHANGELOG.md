## 0.11.0 — unreleased (2026-10-19)

- Added four generative scenes and rebuilt `dsh`:
  - `volcano` **Volcano** (火山爆发): rebuilt as one continuous eruption instead of a loop. The sky starts clear over a compact truncated cone; the eruption then builds a thick, churning canopy about half the screen wide. Its dense middle keeps rolling as fresh ash is supplied and never shrinks, while the flanks stay thinner. Lava bombs and rock blocks rain out of the middle and explode on landing, a continuous fountain feeds the column, two curved lava rivers crawl down the cone, and volcanic lightning crawls through the ash. ASCII by default; `--unicode` uses solid blocks for the mountain and ash.
  - `launch` **Rocket Launch** (火箭发射): a 24-second pad-to-orbit film. The rocket is now a terminal-scaled two-stage stack with twin boosters (roughly 42% of the screen height), drawn from a 7/9/11-column glyph grid. Ignition gets a fireball, shock ring and full-screen flash; the ascent has a layered plume with a white-hot spine and shock diamonds, world-space smoke, steam and sparks, a following camera with shake, a blue-hour sky gradient, parallax stars, max-Q clouds, a MECO flash with tumbling boosters and core stage, a second-stage climb, and an Earth-limb atmosphere with an orbital sunrise. New `--rocket-scale` and `--shake` knobs. Pure ASCII.
  - `plasma` **Plasma** (等离子): nine drifting metaballs, sine texture and a cycling palette. ASCII by default; `--unicode` uses a compact block ramp.
  - `interstellar` **Interstellar** (星际穿越): a 26-second rendezvous at an eight-section ring station. Eight spokes tie the thick ring cabins to a control room whose hexagonal block, windows, gold index and masts all turn with the ring; a three-arm docking claw hangs off the same rotating frame. A compact four-armed ship — one long gold index arm, one engine arm and two shorter side arms — arrives from the upper left, spirals down the upper-left radial while its roll turns with the ring, and meets the hub centre-to-centre at **20.0 s**. Contact throws a spark ring and a short flash; the docked stack then burns away toward a vanishing point with a short, faint trail of station after-images, and collapses into one star that blinks twice. The empty sky then dips to black over half a second and recovers over another half second before the loop restarts. ASCII by default; `--unicode` uses solid-block hull sections. New `docs/interstellar-ascii.png`, `docs/interstellar-storyboard.png` and `docs/interstellar.gif`.
  - `dsh` **DeepSeek Harness** (深度求索) was rebuilt: the wordmark is now two centred lines (`DEEPSEEK` / `HARNESS`) laid out from 6×9 and 8×11 display faces with horizontal pixel doubling. Letter spacing is now a hard layout rule: zero-gap candidates are gone, the 3× face always keeps two blank columns, and the 2× face tries three then two before one. A 248×56 terminal gets a 206×11 lockup with two-column gaps (instead of the old 199×11 one-column merge); 160×48 gets 117×9 with three-column gaps. It picks the largest face that leaves room for a hand-traced **blue whale mark** (20×10 at 160×48, 13×6 at 120×36, 11×5 at 100×30, hidden below 100 columns) can sit just right of the K and fade in after the landing bounce. The intro reveals letters one by one and drops the lockup in quantised one-row steps; when it lands the lockup makes one quick two-row hop back up before settling, while the ghost stack — which belongs to HARNESS alone, starts at the feet of the letters and is kept deliberately faint — stays on the bottom step. The wordmark then stays on screen forever while a narrow diagonal band of light runs from its upper-left corner to its lower-right corner: each glyph lifts from ice blue to white as the band arrives and falls back behind it; the pulse enters and leaves the frame before the next pass. Nothing is drawn between the letters — the movement is a pure font-brightness gradient, not a beam. A small star twinkles at the whale's tail tip, replaying the Interstellar vanishing star's two-blink cross flare every 4.6 seconds. No other fireworks or particle burst; `--trail` still scales the ghosts. The wordmark's own chromatic edge is now painted only into the blank columns beside a glyph, and the glyph colour was moved toward the theme's white; previously the edge overwrote the letters themselves on wide terminals, leaving two of every three stroke columns nearly black. ASCII by default; `--unicode` uses solid blocks. New `docs/dsh-ascii.png`, `docs/dsh-unicode.png` and `docs/dsh.gif`.
- `shanghai` river rework: the Huangpu surface now runs on the `plasma` scene's idea — a set of drifting, breathing metaballs whose summed field is quantised into a dedicated water ramp. The lobes merge, split and slide downstream; cyan and ice currents carry the water, while pink and gold tower and rooftop glows ripple over them, so the palette stays the Bund's night water rather than the plasma theme. The skyline, tower, boats, wake, stars and meteors are unchanged. Both ASCII and Unicode ramps are new, and tests cover the field's motion, the ripple bounds and the absence of the plasma palette.
- `fish` Aquarium: the three big sprites were redrawn as long soft-edged ellipses — rounded `,` / `'` outlines, a dark `%` back, a bright `+` belly and a single pointed `>` snout — instead of the old angular, sawtooth bodies. The forked tail, eye, tail wave and fixed mouth are unchanged; a new test asserts that each body widens and tapers smoothly, and the ASCII / Unicode previews plus the GIF were re-recorded.
- The effect registry now has **32 effects**. Shortcuts are now optional in the metadata: the four newest scenes have no single-key shortcut and are entered with `trix <effect>` or `n` / `p`; all existing shortcuts are unchanged.
- The experimental `lights` **Aurora** (极光) scene was removed before release; it is no longer part of the registry.
- `scripts/check_docs.py` now checks 32 effects. README / README.zh-CN, the HUD document, the help screenshots, the overview and the social preview were updated; tests grew to 254.
